tinyanalytics is a privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics platform for your website and your product. You get the metrics you expect from Google Analytics — visitors, pageviews, sources, funnels, retention — without setting cookies, without a cookie banner, and without handing your visitors' data to a third party. It runs as a hosted service you sign in to at [dash.tinyanalytics.io](https://dash.tinyanalytics.io).

The idea in one line: **understand your traffic and your product without tracking people.**

## What you can measure

tinyanalytics covers three jobs from a single tracking script:

- **Web analytics** — pageviews, sessions, top pages, sources and channels, countries, devices, journeys, retention, funnels, and goals.
- **Product analytics** — custom events, user identification, feature flags, A/B experiments, surveys, behavioral cohorts, revenue, and B2B group analytics.
- **Monitoring** — uptime checks, scheduled email reports, and alerts to email, Slack, Discord, or a webhook.

## How is tinyanalytics private by default?

tinyanalytics counts visitors without cookies and without storing personal data on the visitor's device. Instead of a cookie, each visit is matched to a short, one-way hash derived from the request. Nothing personal is written to the browser, so there is nothing to consent to and nothing to leak.

Because there is no cookie, the visitor count is a close estimate rather than an exact figure — a trade every cookieless tool makes. tinyanalytics is honest about this and gives you [`identify()`](/guides/identify-users) to attach your own stable user IDs when you need people-level accuracy. See [how tinyanalytics works](/how-it-works) for the full mechanism.

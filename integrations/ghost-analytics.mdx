To add tinyanalytics to a **Ghost** site, paste the tracking snippet into the **Site Header** code injection. It applies to every page, works with any theme, and needs no theme editing.

## Before you start

- A tinyanalytics site. If you don't have one yet, follow the [Quickstart](/tinyanalytics-quickstart).
- Your snippet from **Settings → Tracking** in your dashboard.

## Add the snippet to Code injection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Code injection">
    In Ghost admin, go to **Settings → Code injection**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste into Site Header">
    Paste your tinyanalytics snippet into the **Site Header** field:

    ```html
    <script defer src="https://dash.tinyanalytics.io/script.js"
      data-site-id="YOUR_SITE_ID"></script>
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The snippet is now on every page of your published site.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Replace `YOUR_SITE_ID` with your real site ID, and copy the full snippet from **Settings → Tracking** if you've toggled any options.
</Tip>

## Verify

Open your Ghost site in a browser, then check **Realtime** in your dashboard for your visit. See [Verify your setup](/verify-tinyanalytics-installation) for the full check.

<Check>
  Your install is working when your own visit appears in **Realtime** in your dashboard.
</Check>

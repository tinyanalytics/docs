To add tinyanalytics to a **Bubble** app, paste the tracking snippet into the app's header script setting. It applies to every page of your app.

## Before you start

- A tinyanalytics site. If you don't have one yet, follow the [Quickstart](/tinyanalytics-quickstart).
- Your snippet from **Settings → Tracking** in your dashboard.

## Add the snippet to the page header

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open SEO / metatags">
    In the Bubble editor, go to **Settings → SEO / metatags**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste into the header field">
    Paste your tinyanalytics snippet into **Script/meta tags in header**:

    ```html
    <script defer src="https://dash.tinyanalytics.io/script.js"
      data-site-id="YOUR_SITE_ID"></script>
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy to live">
    Save, then **deploy to live** for the change to reach your published app.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Replace `YOUR_SITE_ID` with your real site ID, and copy the full snippet from **Settings → Tracking** if you've toggled any options.
</Tip>

## Single-page navigation is tracked automatically

A Bubble app renders as a single-page app, and tinyanalytics records page changes as pageviews by default (`data-track-spa`). See [Track single-page apps](/configure-tinyanalytics-tracking-script#track-single-page-apps) to change this.

## Verify

Open your deployed Bubble app in a browser, then check **Realtime** in your dashboard for your visit. See [Verify your setup](/verify-tinyanalytics-installation) for the full check.

<Check>
  Your install is working when your own visit appears in **Realtime** in your dashboard.
</Check>

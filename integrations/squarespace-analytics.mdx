To add tinyanalytics to a **Squarespace** site, paste the tracking snippet into the **Header** code injection. It applies to every page of your site.

<Note>
  Code Injection is available on the **Business plan and higher**. On the Personal plan, Squarespace doesn't allow custom header code, so this method isn't available.
</Note>

## Before you start

- A tinyanalytics site. If you don't have one yet, follow the [Quickstart](/tinyanalytics-quickstart).
- Your snippet from **Settings → Tracking** in your dashboard.
- A Squarespace **Business plan or higher**.

## Add the snippet to Code Injection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Code Injection">
    In your Squarespace dashboard, go to **Settings → Advanced → Code Injection**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste into Header">
    Paste your tinyanalytics snippet into the **Header** field:

    ```html
    <script defer src="https://dash.tinyanalytics.io/script.js"
      data-site-id="YOUR_SITE_ID"></script>
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The snippet loads on every page of your site.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Replace `YOUR_SITE_ID` with your real site ID, and copy the full snippet from **Settings → Tracking** if you've toggled any options.
</Tip>

## Verify

Open your Squarespace site in a browser, then check **Realtime** in your dashboard for your visit. See [Verify your setup](/verify-tinyanalytics-installation) for the full check.

<Check>
  Your install is working when your own visit appears in **Realtime** in your dashboard.
</Check>

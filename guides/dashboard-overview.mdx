The Overview is your site's home screen in tinyanalytics. It shows six top-line metrics, a trend chart, breakdown tables, and a map — all scoped to the date range and filters you pick. Everything on the page updates together, so narrowing to one country or one page re-scopes every number at once.

## The six top-line metrics

Each metric is shown for your selected date range, with its change versus the previous period of the same length.

| Metric | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Sessions** | The number of distinct visits. A session is one visitor's activity within a 30-minute sliding window of inactivity. |
| **Pageviews** | The total number of pages viewed. |
| **Users** | The number of distinct people. If you [identify users](/guides/identify-users), this counts your user IDs; otherwise it counts distinct cookieless visitors. |
| **Pages per session** | The average number of pageviews per session. |
| **Bounce rate** | The percentage of sessions with exactly one pageview. |
| **Session duration** | The average time between a session's first and last activity, in seconds. |

<Note>
  A session counts as a bounce when it has **exactly one pageview**, even if the visitor clicked a button or submitted a form on that page.
</Note>

## The trend chart

The chart plots one metric over time. Pick the metric, and choose how to bucket it — by **hour**, **day**, **week**, or **month**. The line is continuous with no gaps: time buckets with no activity show as zero rather than being skipped, so the shape of your traffic is accurate.

## Breakdowns

Below the chart, tabbed breakdown tables rank the top values for a dimension over your date range — **Pages**, **Referrers**, **Geo**, **Devices**, **UTMs**, and **Events**. Each row shows a count and its share of the total.

**Click any row to filter the whole dashboard by that value.** Clicking "United States" in the Geo breakdown, for example, re-scopes every metric, the chart, and the other breakdowns to US visitors. See [filter your data](/guides/filters).

## The visitor map

The map shows where your visitors are, shaded by volume. Like the breakdowns, it's scoped to your current date range and filters.

## Live visitors

The Overview shows how many people are on your site right now — the count of distinct sessions active in the last few minutes. For the live view of who's on the site, open [Realtime](/guides/realtime).

## Date range and comparison

Pick a date range from the presets or a custom range. Ranges are calculated in **your browser's time zone**, so "today" means your today. Turn on comparison to see each metric against the previous period of the same length, so you can tell whether a number is up or down.

Realtime shows who's on your site right now. The live count is the number of **distinct sessions** active in the last few minutes, and it counts people rather than events — a visitor loading ten pages still counts as one. The number decays smoothly as visitors go idle, so it always reflects current activity.

## How the live count works

- It counts **distinct sessions** seen in the last five minutes, so one active visitor is one, no matter how many pages they load.
- It counts **people, not bots** — traffic identified as bots is filtered out before it reaches the live count.
- It **decays gradually**. As each visitor goes quiet, their session ages out of the window, so the count falls one visitor at a time rather than dropping all at once.

## Live sessions

Alongside the count, the live sessions view lists the individual sessions active in the last few minutes — where each visitor came from, their location and device, and the pages they're on. Open a session to see its full timeline in [Sessions](/guides/sessions).

## When Realtime is most useful

- **Confirming an install.** After you add the tracking snippet, visit your own site and watch your session appear. See [verify your setup](/guides/verify-your-setup).
- **Watching a launch or campaign.** See traffic arrive live when you publish a post, send an email, or go live on a channel.
- **Spotting a spike.** Notice a surge as it happens, then switch to the [Overview](/guides/dashboard-overview) to break it down by source.
